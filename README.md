# Awesome-Environmental-Health-n-Safety-Platform

## Top Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Environmental, Health & Safety Management, Workplace Safety, Risk, Compliance & Sustainability*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Environmental Health & Safety (EHS)**. These tools help organizations manage workplace safety, environmental compliance, incidents, inspections, audits, risk assessments, corrective actions, occupational health, chemicals, permits, training, sustainability, and regulatory compliance.

**Examples** include Cority, Intelex, Sphera, VelocityEHS, EcoOnline, Benchmark Gensuite, Enablon, Evotix, ETQ Reliance, Ideagen EHS, IsoMetrix, Origami Risk, Quentic, and Safetymint.

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with dedicated open-source EHS/HSE platforms, safety management systems, hazard-management tools, HSE calculators, environmental-management projects, and reusable infrastructure for building self-hosted EHS solutions — ideal for organizations, researchers, safety professionals, and developers seeking transparent and customizable alternatives.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

* **[BeaconHS](https://github.com/braedonsaunders/beaconhs)**
  Open-source multi-tenant HSE platform for industrial construction covering incidents, inspections, training, equipment, PPE, permits, corrective actions, lone-worker safety, confined spaces, lift plans, and toolbox talks.

* **[Autonomous EHS Management](https://github.com/SafetyMP/Autonomous-EHS-Management)**
  Self-hostable open-source EHS console covering incidents, CAPA, audits, inspections, observations, work permits, metrics, and compliance-oriented workflows.

* **[SafetIbase](https://github.com/safetibase/safetibase)**
  Open-source construction health and safety system for identifying, managing, and communicating hazards throughout construction projects.

* **[HSE Digital Toolkit](https://github.com/UmairPashaa/HSE-Digital-Toolkit)**
  Open-source, offline-capable HSE toolkit containing risk assessments, PPE checklists, incident reporting, inspections, toolbox talks, emergency contacts, CAPA, audits, and environmental tools.

* **[HSE Calculators](https://github.com/SmartQHSE/hse-calculators)**
  Open-source TypeScript library containing 30+ HSE calculators including TRIR, LTIR, LTIFR, DART, EMR, severity rates, heat stress, noise exposure, vibration, ergonomics, fire safety, risk matrices, and carbon-footprint calculations.

* **[HSE Calculators Python](https://github.com/SmartQHSE/hse-calculators-py)**
  Python implementation of open-source HSE calculation functions covering incident rates, occupational exposure, ergonomics, risk assessment, fire safety, and other EHS metrics.

* **[Awesome HSE](https://github.com/SmartQHSE/awesome-hse)**
  Open curated directory of HSE software, regulations, datasets, tools, books, training resources, and related open-source projects.

* **[Kumu HSE / Safety Management Projects](https://github.com/topics/safety-management)**
  GitHub ecosystem containing open-source safety-management projects for incident analysis, corrective actions, risk assessment, hazard identification, and workplace safety.

* **[OSHA-AI](https://github.com/Osha-AI/Osha-AI)**

* **[Open EHS](https://github.com/open-ehs/open-ehs)**

* **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**

* **[Frappe Framework](https://github.com/frappe/frappe)**

* **[Odoo Community](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**

* **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)**

  Open-source weather API that can provide meteorological data for heat-stress monitoring, environmental risk assessment, outdoor-work safety, and operational planning.

  Open environmental data infrastructure that can be integrated into environmental compliance, air-quality monitoring, and occupational exposure workflows.

  Open-source security compliance framework that can provide a technical compliance layer for organizations integrating IT security controls into broader EHS/GRC programs.

* **[Open Policy Agent](https://github.com/open-policy-agent/opa)**
  Open-source policy engine useful for implementing machine-readable compliance rules, approval policies, and automated governance controls.

* **[Keycloak](https://github.com/keycloak/keycloak)**
  Open-source identity and access-management platform useful for securing self-hosted EHS systems with SSO, roles, groups, and centralized authentication.

* **[PostgreSQL](https://github.com/postgres/postgres)**
  Open-source relational database suitable as the system of record for custom self-hosted EHS, incident, audit, inspection, risk, and environmental-management applications.

## Disclaimer

* This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
* EHS software capabilities, pricing, licensing, and availability can change over time.
* Some projects listed as open-source are complete EHS applications, while others are specialized components or building blocks that can be combined into a self-hosted EHS platform.
* Open-source software does not automatically provide regulatory certification or compliance. Organizations remain responsible for validating workflows, calculations, records, controls, and reporting against applicable laws and standards.
* Safety-critical and environmental compliance systems should be properly secured, validated, tested, backed up, and reviewed by qualified EHS professionals.
